How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to neerajmshr98@gmail.com
  2. Use subject: Astro AI Account Deletion Request

We will verify ownership and process your request.

What gets deleted

  • Your Astro AI account record (Google ID, name, email, profile photo URL)
  • Phone number saved in the app
  • Chat sessions / message history linked to your account
  • Language preference and related app account data

What may be kept temporarily

  • Limited server/security logs may be kept for up to 30 days for fraud prevention and legal compliance, then removed or anonymized
  • Data already processed by third-party providers (e.g. Google Sign-In / Gemini) is subject to those providers’ policies

Timeline

We aim to delete your account and associated app data within 7 days of verifying your request. You will receive a confirmation email when done.

Uninstall note

Uninstalling Astro AI from your phone does not automatically delete your account from our servers. Use the email request above to delete your account and data.
